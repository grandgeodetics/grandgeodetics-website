Privacy, plainly

What this website and Land Studio do with information about you.

No tracking cookiesNo advertsNo accounts

This website

The website has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and the country and browser of visitors without cookies and without identifying you. Download numbers come from GitHub, where the installers are published.

The contact form does not send anything to us by itself: it opens your own e-mail app with the message filled in, and you decide whether to send it. When you e-mail us, we use your address only to answer you.

The Verify page checks a plan's QR code on your own device. If you use the camera, the picture stays on your phone and is never uploaded; the code you scan is not sent to us. The list of issued plans it checks holds no names, places or coordinates.

The training request form, like the contact form, opens your own e-mail app or WhatsApp with the request filled in. Tutorial videos hosted on YouTube are shown through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

The geoportal loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap when you choose that background, so OpenStreetMap receives the map area you view, as with any web map.

Land Studio

Land Studio keeps your projects and settings on your computer. Grand Geodetics does not receive your project data through the app. Optional online features (open data downloads, the registry account, the Gemini assistant with your own key, and the update check) send requests to those services when you use them. Details are in sections 6 and 7 of the licence agreement.

If you process personal data with Land Studio, such as names and land claims, you are responsible for doing so lawfully under the Data Protection Act, 2024 of Malawi.
