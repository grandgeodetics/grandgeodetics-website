Open Malawi map data

The base layers that ship with Land Studio, free to download. Coordinates are WGS 84 longitude and latitude (EPSG:4326); Land Studio and QGIS reproject them to Arc 1950 / UTM zone 36S on loading.

Using the data

When you publish a map made with these layers, credit the source: "Boundaries: geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0)" and "Lakes, rivers, roads and towns: Natural Earth". Administrative boundaries are shown for reference and do not represent an official position on any boundary.

Rivers, roads, the railway and towns come from Natural Earth at 1:10 million scale: good for an overview of the country, not for site work. More detailed layers, protected areas and flood-prone areas will be added here as they are prepared.
