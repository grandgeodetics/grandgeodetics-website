How it works

Every sketch plan made with Land Studio carries a QR code with its reference, map sheet, area, beacons and a fingerprint. The fingerprint is calculated from the plan's coordinates, so changing a beacon or the area changes it.

The land office sends Grand Geodetics the list of plans it has issued, and Grand Geodetics signs that list. This page checks the signature first, so a copied or edited list is refused.

Your privacy

The list holds only references, fingerprints, sheets, areas, beacon counts, dates and status. It holds no names, villages or coordinates. The check runs on your phone; the code you scan is never sent to us or anyone else.

If a plan does not match

Do not rely on it. Contact the land office that issued it, quoting the plan reference, or contact us.
